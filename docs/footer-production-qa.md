# SURA Production Footer and Access QA

The public home page was reviewed at 1280 × 720 and 390 × 844 after the production-footer refinement. The updated footer presents SURA’s considered-local-plan positioning, Terms, Privacy, a secure email-account entry point, and accessible X and Instagram links. The social destinations are intentionally generic platform destinations and are labelled as awaiting confirmed SURA handles; no unverified brand account was fabricated.

The mobile review retains the footer links in the reading order after public content and preserves the email-account navigation. Desktop and mobile private-entry tests verify that non-email legacy sessions are rejected, while verified Supabase email sessions retain access to the existing SURA ownership model.
