# SURA Visual-Identity Validation

The revised identity is grounded in original SURA assets and Kenya-relevant research, without representing generated visual studies as real company products or customer work.

| Area | Validated outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Brand language | The public lock-up now reads **SURA / LOCAL EDITS**. Public discovery uses **Discover companies**, replacing the ambiguous “Find local form.” The footer motto is **Local living, composed with care.** |
| Theme treatment | Each of the twelve aesthetics sets accessible colour tokens plus display, body, and kicker font variables. The provider test confirms a theme change updates both colour and typography tokens. |
| Visual studies | Three original illustrations—Sisal & terracotta, Ink & ivory, and Thermal bloom—appear with an explicit “Illustrative study · not inventory” label and supporting explanation. They do not claim connection to listed companies. |
| Desktop review | The revised home page was reviewed at 1280 × 900. The company-discovery wording, study labels, and cards remain clearly differentiated from the adjacent demo-maker directory. |
| Mobile review | The revised home page was reviewed at 390 × 844. Navigation, editorial hierarchy, stacked study cards, labels, and demo-company cards remain readable and non-overlapping. |

The reference sources and safeguards are recorded in [`kenya-visual-reference-research.md`](./kenya-visual-reference-research.md). Company imagery remains a verified-seller responsibility; SURA’s studies are only a visual language reference.
