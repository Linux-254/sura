# SURA Aesthetic Direction Research

SURA’s member preferences should be treated as a **mix of directions**, not as a fixed label. This lets a person choose, for example, the grounded warmth of *Savanna Atelier*, the quiet restraint of *Ink & Ivory*, and the celebratory lift of *Tangerine Social* without reducing their expression to one look. The catalogue uses colour harmony as an interface framework—monochromatic, analogous, complementary, triadic, and split-complementary relationships—and retains high-contrast foreground/background pairings for readable controls.[1]

Kenyan inspiration is approached with care: the directions reference materials, landscape, light, and contemporary making rather than claiming a culture as costume. Kenyan design commentary highlights locally meaningful sources such as kanga textiles, Maasai shuka colour, wood, sisal, stone, earthy savanna tones, Swahili architectural detail, and contemporary-traditional blending.[2]

Current global colour commentary supports both gentle, naturally desaturated directions (dusty pink, chalky blue, muted olive, pale terracotta) and sparing high-energy accents, while also showing the renewed relevance of layered brown palettes and confident black-and-gold contrast.[3] SURA uses those signals as optional artistic expressions; it does not make trend participation a requirement for good taste.

## Product decisions

During onboarding, a signed-in member can choose **one to five** directions. Their first choice becomes the active interface palette; the full mix drives a personalised “expression board” of colour accents, material notes, and styling prompts. Members can revise this later in their account. The preference system must remain supportive, never score taste, and must preserve accessible text contrast regardless of the selected mix.

## Implementation validation

The signed-in expression board was visually reviewed at **1280 × 900** and **390 × 844**. The desktop route presents a single, non-duplicated curation board with twelve distinct tonal swatches. The mobile route retains individual touch targets, the current blend, and the save action in one readable vertical flow. Automated validation covers the one-to-five limit, duplicate rejection, supported-direction validation, local preference persistence, and primary-palette application.
