# SURA Messaging

## Core promise

**Make the feeling findable.** Discover a direction, meet the people who make it real, and move from visual signal to a considered next step.

## Company position

**SURA is Africa’s visual network.** It is the company building the layer between visual identity and useful action: see a signal, shape a direction, meet the African people and businesses behind it, and move with trust.

The ambition is category-defining scale, not an imitation of another social platform. SURA should become the place where Africa’s aesthetics, creators, businesses, culture and everyday choices become discoverable, connected and economically useful.

## Launch hooks

- Your taste is not the problem. The search is.
- A moodboard is a beginning. SURA helps it move.
- Nairobi has the makers. SURA gives the feeling a route.
- What if your budget could become a point of view?
- Stop saving the feeling. Start shaping it.

## CTA

Primary: **Enter the signal**
Secondary: **List your business**
Business: **Put your point of view to work**

Company: **Build the network Africa sees itself in**

## Caption structure

Hook in the first line. Name the friction in one sentence. Show the SURA loop: see → shape → source → handoff. End with one CTA only. Use 5–8 relevant hashtags, with `#SURA` and `#KenyanDesign` as anchors when appropriate.
