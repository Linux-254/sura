# SURA ICP

SURA is for style-aware people in Kenya who can see what feels right but want a clearer way to source it, plan it and meet trustworthy businesses. They may be building a room, editing a wardrobe, finding a maker, choosing a salon, planning an occasion, commissioning creative work or simply collecting the details that make life feel coherent.

The second audience is the business owner: a local creative, shop, studio, maker, stylist, hospitality operator, detailer, venue, groomer or service provider who wants their point of view represented with more context than a directory listing.

Their key tension is not lack of taste; it is fragmented discovery, unclear pricing and low trust. SURA answers with visual context, honest affordability bands, local relevance, structured onboarding and a visible order handoff.
